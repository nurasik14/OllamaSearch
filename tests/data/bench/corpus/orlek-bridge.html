Orlek Bridge

The Orlek Bridge is a cable-stayed road bridge crossing the Saryk river gorge. It was built by Tamarisk Engineering and opened to traffic in 2004.

The bridge is 1,250 metres long and carries four lanes of traffic. Engineers chose a cable-stayed design because of strong seasonal winds in the gorge, which made a suspension bridge too costly.

A small museum at the eastern abutment documents the construction with photographs and models.
