Zarnitsa-on-Ural

Zarnitsa-on-Ural is a small river town on the western bank of the Ural, known for its wooden merchant houses and its spring kite festival.

According to the 2023 census the town has a population of 7,420 people. The current mayor is Dinara Oskembay, elected in 2021.

The town was founded in 1856 as a river crossing and grew around a salt warehouse. Today the local economy depends on fishing, small boat repair and seasonal tourism along the river.
