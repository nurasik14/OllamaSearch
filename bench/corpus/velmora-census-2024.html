2024 census: preliminary results for the towns of Darvesh Province

Darvesh Provincial Statistics Office - Statistical Bulletin No. 41 - published 14 October 2024

The Darvesh Provincial Statistics Office today published the preliminary results of the 2024 census for the three largest towns of the province. Enumeration took place between 1 and 30 April 2024 and covered all permanent residents. Final figures, including age structure and household size, will follow in the spring.

According to the 2024 census, the population of Velmora is 19,835, up from 18,460 in 2019. The increase of 1,375 residents is the largest relative growth among the towns of the province and is attributed mainly to new housing in the Upper Ford district and to the expansion of local industry.

The provincial capital Saryzhal remains the largest town with 31,207 residents (2019: 30,118). Ostravel grew more slowly and now counts 8,912 residents, compared with 8,640 five years ago.
