Darvesh Province is a province in the river lowlands between the Kumdala Hills in the south and the open steppe in the north. Its administrative centre is Saryzhal. The province is crossed by two large rivers, the Isvelka and the Myrna, which together drain most of its territory.

The province has a population of about 1.3 million, of whom roughly a third live in towns. Besides Saryzhal, the main towns are Velmora, known for its glass and optics industry, and Ostravel, a historic market town on the Myrna. Agriculture remains important: wheat, sunflower and sugar beet are grown on the black-earth plains, and sheep are raised on the drier southern pastures.

The governor of Darvesh Province is Askar Tulegen. The provincial assembly has 45 members elected for five-year terms. The province is divided into eleven districts.

Tourism is modest but growing. Visitors come for the salt-caravan heritage of Velmora, the wooden market hall of Ostravel, and hiking in the Kumdala Hills, where the Zhelmaya Cave with its prehistoric paintings is the best-known attraction.
