Teodor Malkam

Teodor Malkam is a folk-rock singer and guitarist, famous for his gravelly voice and long, storytelling songs about fishermen, salt caravans and river towns. He has been touring for more than twenty years and is one of the best-loved live performers on the coast.

Malkam's debut album, Northbound Salt, was released in 2009. Recorded in a week in a converted net loft, it contains the song "Caravan to Velmora", which remains his most requested number. He followed it with Harbour Lights Down (2012) and The Long Ferry (2016).

He has played at most of the major festivals in the region and is known for concerts lasting over three hours. In 2020 he published a memoir about his years on the road. Malkam lives in a fishing village near Morvenn with his family.

Critics often compare his songwriting to the old ballad tradition of the coast: verses that tell a complete story, choruses that audiences can learn after one hearing, and lyrics full of place names, boats and weather. His backing band has barely changed in two decades and includes an accordion, a fiddle and a double bass. Malkam has said that he still writes every song on the same second-hand guitar that he bought as a teenager with money earned gutting herring in the harbour.

Beyond music, he has campaigned for the protection of small fishing harbours and has organised benefit concerts for the families of lost fishermen.
