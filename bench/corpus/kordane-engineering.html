About Kordane Engineering

Kordane Engineering is a civil engineering company specialising in long-span bridges, hydraulic structures and rail infrastructure. The company was founded in 1987 in Saryzhal by the structural engineer Henrik Kordane, who had previously worked for the provincial roads department, together with four colleagues and a rented drawing office above a bakery.

In its first years the firm designed small road bridges and culverts for the farming districts of Darvesh Province. Its breakthrough came in the 1990s, when it won its first large international design competition. Since then Kordane Engineering has completed the Myrna River flood barrier (2004), the Halvorsk ring road interchange (2011) and the modernisation of the Saryzhal rail depot (2019).

Today the company is led by managing director Alua Zhanserik and remains headquartered in Saryzhal, with regional offices in Halvorsk and Kelstrand. Our engineers combine traditional structural expertise with digital design tools, wind-tunnel testing and long-term monitoring of the structures we build.
