Morvenn Lights Festival 2019

The Morvenn Lights Festival is an annual festival of light, music and lanterns held in the old harbour of the coastal town of Morvenn at the end of August. The 2019 edition ran over four nights and was the last festival before the event was cancelled in 2020 and 2021.

A total of 34,200 visitors attended the 2019 Morvenn Lights Festival, according to the organisers' final report. The programme included the traditional floating of paper lanterns on the harbour basin, a parade of illuminated fishing boats, light installations in the narrow lanes of the old town and a fireworks display over the breakwater.

The headliner of the 2019 festival was the folk-rock singer Teodor Malkam, who played a three-hour set on the main stage on the Saturday night. Other performers included the Morvenn Harbour Brass Band, several choirs from the region and a children's lantern orchestra.

The weather was kind for most of the weekend, apart from a short thunderstorm on the Thursday evening that delayed the opening ceremony by an hour. Organisers had extended the festival area along the north quay for the first time, adding a food market with stalls from fishing villages along the coast. Extra ferries from Kelstrand and special late buses helped visitors return home after the fireworks.

The organisers announced that the festival would continue to be free of charge, funded by local businesses, the town council and the sale of lanterns.
