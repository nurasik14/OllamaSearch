Record crowds as the 2022 Morvenn Lights Festival returns to the harbour

By the Courier culture desk - published 22 August 2022

After two years of cancellations, the Morvenn Lights Festival returned to the old harbour this weekend, and the town turned out in force. Organisers said that 41,750 visitors attended the 2022 festival over its four nights, the highest figure in the event's history.

Thousands of paper lanterns were floated on the harbour basin on Friday, and the illuminated boat parade on Saturday drew crowds ten deep along the quays. The headliner of the 2022 festival, singer-songwriter Ilsa Varnhout, closed the main stage on Saturday night with a set that ended with the whole harbour singing along.

Festival director Ruth Akhmetova-Lane said the success showed how much the town had missed the event. "We planned for 35,000 and we got far more," she said. Local traders reported their busiest weekend since the festival was last held. The 2023 dates will be announced in the spring.
