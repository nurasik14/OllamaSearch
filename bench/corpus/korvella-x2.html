Korvella X2 hands-on: a sensible phone with stamina

The Korvella X2 is a mid-range smartphone from Tessaric Devices, released in September 2023. It does not try to impress with gimmicks; instead it focuses on a good screen, a dependable camera and a battery that lasts.

The battery capacity of the Korvella X2 is 4,850 mAh. In our tests it comfortably lasted a full day of heavy use and about a day and a half with moderate use. Charging is limited to 33 W, so a full charge takes a little over an hour.

The 6.3-inch OLED display is sharp and bright, and the phone weighs a pleasant 186 g. The 50-megapixel main camera produces good results in daylight, although night shots are noisier than on more expensive models.

Software is one of the phone's strengths. Tessaric Devices ships a lightly modified version of the operating system with few pre-installed apps, and the company has promised four years of major system updates and five years of security patches. The fingerprint reader under the display is fast and reliable, and the stereo speakers are louder than we expected at this price.

There are compromises: the frame is plastic rather than metal, there is no wireless charging, and the ultra-wide camera is mediocre. The phone is rated for splash resistance only, so it should not be taken swimming.

At a launch price of 429 euros, the Korvella X2 is easy to recommend for anyone who values battery life over the latest features. Buyers who want even more endurance and a zoom camera should look at the more expensive Pro model released later.
