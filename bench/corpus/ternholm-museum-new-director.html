Matthias Lindqvaal appointed director of the Ternholm Museum of Steppe Art

Press release - Halvorsk, 1 September 2022

The board of trustees of the Ternholm Museum of Steppe Art announces that Matthias Lindqvaal has taken up the post of director of the museum with effect from 1 September 2022. He replaces Gaukhar Temirbek, who led the museum for eleven years and is retiring.

Lindqvaal comes to Halvorsk from the Kelstrand Maritime Museum, where he was deputy director and curator of a series of acclaimed exhibitions on coastal trade. He studied art history and has published widely on textiles and decorative arts.

"Gaukhar Temirbek leaves a museum in excellent shape, with a new exhibition wing and a collection that is fully catalogued online," said the chair of the board. "We are delighted that Matthias Lindqvaal will build on this work and bring the collection to new audiences." Lindqvaal said his priorities would be education programmes for schools and closer cooperation with village museums across the region.

Among his first projects will be a touring exhibition of felt carpets and embroidery from the museum's collection, which will travel to schools and community centres in smaller towns. Lindqvaal also intends to extend the museum's opening hours on weekday evenings and to develop a programme of concerts and lectures in the courtyard of the merchant house during the summer months.

The board thanked the museum staff for their work during the transition and invited members of the public to a reception in honour of the outgoing director later this month.
