Velmora Optics: a century of glass from the Isvelka valley

Company profile - updated by the DBR companies desk

Velmora Optics is a manufacturer of precision lenses, binoculars and survey instruments with its headquarters in the town of Velmora in Darvesh Province. The company was founded in 1931 by the glass grinder Benedikt Orsza, who began with a two-room workshop next to the old salt warehouses on Quay Street and a single grinding machine bought on credit.

Orsza's first commercial success was a range of surveying levels for the provincial land registry. During the 1950s the firm moved into binoculars and camera lenses, and in the 1970s it opened its present factory in the Upper Ford district. Today the product range includes the Arctur binocular series, theodolites for construction firms and the VX-9 telephoto lens, launched in 2021 and widely praised by wildlife photographers.

The company is led by chief executive Tomas Rellgard, who took over in 2016 after a career in industrial ceramics. Under Rellgard the firm has concentrated on high-end lenses and exports, which now account for most of its sales. Velmora Optics remains privately owned; a foundation set up by the Orsza family holds the majority of the shares.
