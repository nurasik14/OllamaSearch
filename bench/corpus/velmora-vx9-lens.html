VX-9 150-600 mm telephoto zoom

The VX-9 is a long telephoto zoom lens produced by Velmora Optics for wildlife, sports and aviation photography. It was launched in 2021 and is assembled at the company's Upper Ford factory.

Focal length150-600 mm
Maximum aperturef/5-6.3
Optical design24 elements in 16 groups, 4 low-dispersion elements
Weight2,140 g
Filter diameter95 mm
Lead designerIngrid Saarvald

The optical design of the VX-9 was led by Ingrid Saarvald, who joined the company's design office from a university optics laboratory. Her team used four low-dispersion glass elements melted in Velmora to suppress colour fringing at the long end of the zoom range, a weak point of many lenses in this class.

The lens body is made of magnesium alloy and is sealed against dust and rain. A rotating tripod collar, a zoom lock and a three-position focus limiter are standard. At 2,140 g the VX-9 is heavier than some competitors, but reviewers have praised its sharpness at 600 mm and its quiet autofocus motor.

The VX-9 is available in mounts for the major camera systems. A matching 1.4x teleconverter was introduced in 2022.
