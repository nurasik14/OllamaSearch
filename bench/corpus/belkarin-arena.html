Belkarin Arena

Belkarin Arena is a football and athletics stadium on the western edge of the town of Dorvast. It was opened in 1972 on the site of a former brickworks and takes its name from the Belkarin hill that overlooks it.

After a major renovation completed in 2006, which removed the running track from the main stands and added a roof over all four sides, the stadium has a seating capacity of 23,400. The record attendance, set before the renovation when standing terraces were still in use, is 38,000.

The arena has hosted national cup finals, international youth matches and concerts. A small museum under the main stand tells the history of football in the town. The stadium is a ten-minute walk from Dorvast railway station.

The original stadium was designed by the provincial architecture bureau as a classic bowl with an athletics track and open terraces on three sides. Its most distinctive feature, the cantilevered concrete roof over the main stand, was considered daring at the time and has since been listed as a monument of post-war architecture. During the 2006 renovation the roof was preserved and new steel roofs were added over the other three stands in a matching profile.

The pitch is heated, and floodlights were replaced with energy-efficient LED lighting in 2019.
