Kelstrand

Kelstrand is a port city at the mouth of the Varra estuary. Once dominated by shipbuilding and fish processing, it has reinvented itself over the last two decades as a centre for electronics, software and design. The old shipyards on the river front now house research campuses, start-up incubators and a maritime museum.

The city has a population of about 640,000 and is the main seaport of the region. Container terminals on the western bank handle grain exports from the inland provinces, and car ferries sail to the coastal towns, including Morvenn.

Kelstrand is the home of several technology companies, including the consumer electronics firm Tessaric Devices. The city's technical university works closely with industry, and a science park opened in 2015 on the site of the former naval dockyard.

The transformation was not painless. When the last large shipyard closed in the late 1990s, unemployment in the city rose sharply and whole districts of workers' housing emptied. The city responded by offering cheap space in the empty yards to young companies and by expanding its technical university. Within fifteen years the number of jobs in electronics and software exceeded the number once employed in shipbuilding.

The climate is maritime, with mild winters, cool summers and frequent fog in spring. Strong westerly winds make the estuary popular with sailors and kite-surfers.

The historic centre, with its brick warehouses and canals, was largely restored in the 1990s. The annual Estuary Jazz Festival takes place in July.
