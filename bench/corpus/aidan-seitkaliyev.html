Aidan Seitkaliyev is a middle-distance runner who specialises in the 1500 metres. He was born in Ostravel, a market town on the Myrna River, and started running with the local Myrna Running Club at the age of eleven, training on the towpath along the river.

His breakthrough came at the 2019 Altaran Games, where he won the 1500 m gold medal with a sprint finish over the last 200 metres. He has since competed at several continental championships and is known as a tactical racer with a strong finishing kick.

Seitkaliyev studied sports science part-time and works with young athletes in Ostravel during the off-season. He is coached by Valentina Orlo, a former steeplechaser.

Seitkaliyev trains for most of the year in Ostravel, running long easy miles along the Myrna towpath and doing interval sessions on the old cinder track behind the town school. In winter he spends several weeks at altitude camps in the mountains. He has spoken openly about the difficulty of combining elite sport with work, and about the support of local businesses in Ostravel that sponsored his early travel to competitions.

Away from the track he is a keen chess player and an avid supporter of football in his home province.
