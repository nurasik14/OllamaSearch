About the museum

The Ternholm Museum of Steppe Art was opened in 1964 in the former Ternholm merchant house on Embankment Street in the city of Halvorsk. It was named after the merchant family whose collection of silver, felt and painting formed the core of the museum, and it has since become the largest museum of steppe art in the region.

The collection of the Ternholm Museum now comprises 14,600 works, ranging from Bronze Age gold ornaments and felt carpets to twentieth-century painting and contemporary photography. About 1,200 works are on permanent display in 28 galleries; the rest can be viewed by appointment in the study centre.

The museum is directed by Gaukhar Temirbek, an art historian who previously headed the department of textiles. Under her leadership the museum opened a new wing for temporary exhibitions and launched an online catalogue of the entire collection.
