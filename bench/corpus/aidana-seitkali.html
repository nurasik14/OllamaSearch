Aidana Seitkali

Dr. Aidana Seitkali is a glaciologist known for long-term field measurements of mountain glaciers in the high ranges south of Darvesh Province. She is a professor of physical geography at Kestrin University, where she leads the Mountain Cryosphere Group.

Early life

Aidana Seitkali was born in Saryzhal, the capital of Darvesh Province, and grew up in a family of schoolteachers. As a teenager she joined summer expeditions of the provincial young naturalists' club to the Kumdala Hills, which she later described as the beginning of her interest in landscapes shaped by ice.

Research

Seitkali's best-known work concerns the Ushbel Glacier, the source of the Isvelka River. Her group has maintained a network of stakes, weather stations and time-lapse cameras on the glacier for more than two decades, and has documented its retreat in detail. She has argued that the meltwater of the Ushbel Glacier will peak within the next thirty years, with consequences for farming along the Isvelka.

She has also worked on ice cores and on the use of drones to map crevasse fields, and she regularly advises the provincial water authority.

Awards

In 2021 she received the Brandt-Ishimova Prize for mountain research. She is a member of the regional academy of sciences.
