Tessaric Devices is a consumer electronics company that designs smartphones, e-readers and wearable devices. It was founded in 2009 by a group of former telecom engineers and is headquartered in the city of Kelstrand, where its research campus occupies a former shipyard on the river front.

The company first became known for rugged e-readers with long battery life. It entered the smartphone market in 2016 and now sells its phones in more than thirty countries. Its smartphone line is positioned in the upper mid-range and is known for large batteries and a clean, lightly modified operating system.

The company's early e-readers were designed for outdoor use, with waterproof cases and screens that could be read in bright sunlight, and they sold well among students and travellers. Its first smartphone, released in 2016, was a commercial disappointment, but later models gained a loyal following thanks to their battery life and long software support. In recent years Tessaric Devices has also launched smartwatches and wireless earbuds.

Tessaric Devices is led by chief executive Joakim Feldvaag. In 2024 the company reported revenue of 4.7 billion euros. Manufacturing is outsourced to contract partners, while design, software and testing take place in Kelstrand.
