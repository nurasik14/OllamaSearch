Korvella X2 Pro review: the big battery phone we have been waiting for

By the GadgetSteppe phones team - Rating: 4.5 out of 5

The Korvella X2 Pro is the new flagship smartphone from Tessaric Devices, launched in March 2025. It keeps the clean design of the standard X2 but adds a larger 6.7-inch display, a periscope zoom camera and, most importantly, a much bigger battery.

The battery capacity of the Korvella X2 Pro is 5,320 mAh. In our looping video test the phone lasted 23 hours and 40 minutes, and in everyday use we regularly finished the second day with power to spare. Charging at 65 W takes the phone from empty to full in about 48 minutes.

Performance is excellent, the display is bright enough for direct sunlight, and the main camera produces natural colours. Our main criticisms are the weight of 221 g and the lack of a headphone jack. At its launch price of 749 euros the Korvella X2 Pro is not cheap, but it is the endurance champion of its class.

Battery5,320 mAh, 65 W charging
Weight221 g
