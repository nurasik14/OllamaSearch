Kestrin Technical College

Kestrin Technical College is a vocational and technical college in Halvorsk. It was founded in 1958 to train technicians for the growing machine-building and chemical industries of Orda-Ken Province, and it took its name from the street on which its first building stood, Kestrin Street.

The college offers two- and three-year programmes in mechanical engineering, electrical installation, welding, laboratory technology and information technology. About 3,800 students are enrolled, many of them in apprenticeship programmes run together with local employers.

In 2017 the college opened a new workshop building with modern CNC machines and a training centre for renewable-energy technicians. Graduates of Kestrin Technical College can continue their studies at universities in the region, and many find jobs in the industrial parks around Halvorsk.

The college campus lies in the eastern industrial district, about twenty minutes by tram from the city centre. Besides the workshops it has a student hostel for about 600 students from the villages of the province, a sports hall and a canteen run by students of the catering programme. Each spring the college holds a skills competition in which teams of students build and present working machines, and the winners represent the province at national vocational contests.
