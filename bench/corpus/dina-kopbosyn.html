Dina Kopbosyn

Mayor of Halvorsk (since 2021)

Dina Kopbosyn is an urban planner and politician who has served as mayor of Halvorsk since 2021. Before entering politics she worked for fifteen years in the city's planning department, where she was responsible for the redesign of the central embankment along the Tobyl-Su canal.

Kopbosyn was elected as an independent candidate with the support of several local civic groups. Her programme focused on public transport, green spaces and the restoration of historic merchant houses. During her first term the city introduced electric buses on its main routes and converted two car parks into public gardens.

Kopbosyn studied architecture and urban planning and began her career designing public squares for small towns in Orda-Ken Province. In the planning department of Halvorsk she became known for long public consultations, in which residents were invited to vote on alternative designs for parks and streets. Supporters credit her with making city planning more open; critics say her consultations have slowed down several important building projects.

She has been critical of plans for a new motorway through the eastern districts and has instead promoted a light-rail link to the airport. Kopbosyn is married and has two children.
