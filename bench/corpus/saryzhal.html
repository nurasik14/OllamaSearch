Saryzhal

Saryzhal is the administrative centre of Darvesh Province and its largest town. It stands on a low plateau above the Isvelka River, where the old caravan road from the Kumdala Hills meets the railway to the north. The name is usually explained as "yellow mane", after the tall feather grass that once covered the plateau.

The town grew quickly after the railway arrived in 1896. Much of the old wooden centre was lost in the Great Saryzhal Fire of 1911, and the town was rebuilt in brick with wide streets and a central boulevard lined with poplars. The provincial government, the regional court and the provincial archive are all located on Boulevard Square.

Economy

Saryzhal is the province's centre of trade and services. Engineering firms, including Kordane Engineering, have their headquarters here, and a large wholesale market supplies the surrounding farming districts. The town is also a hub for regional buses and freight.

Culture and education

The Saryzhal Drama Theatre, the provincial museum of local history and a branch of the conservatory make the town the cultural centre of Darvesh Province. The annual Feather Grass Days festival in June features horse games, folk music and a crafts market.
