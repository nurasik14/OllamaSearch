2019 Altaran Games: athletics highlights

The 2019 Altaran Games, a multi-sport competition for athletes from the steppe and coastal regions, were held in Kelstrand from 2 to 11 August 2019. More than 2,600 athletes took part in 24 sports.

The athletics programme took place at the newly renovated Kelstrand Stadium. One of the most exciting races was the men's 1500 m final, in which a pack of seven runners was still together with one lap to go. The race was decided on the final straight.

In the women's events, the 10,000 m was won in a Games record, and the high jump competition went to a jump-off for the first time in the history of the Games. The host city finished top of the medal table in athletics.

The opening ceremony on the harbour front was watched by an estimated 45,000 spectators, and the parade of athletes was led by the flag-bearers of the steppe provinces on horseback. More than 4,100 volunteers helped to run the Games, from transport drivers to interpreters, and the athletes' village in the former naval barracks was later converted into student housing.

Organisers described the 2019 Games as the most successful in their history. Ticket sales exceeded expectations, the new tram line to the stadium carried more than half a million passengers during the ten days, and several venues were reused for national championships in the following years.

The next Altaran Games are scheduled to be held in Halvorsk.
