FC Dorvast

FC Dorvast is a professional football club from the town of Dorvast, playing in the Premier Division. The club was founded in 1949 by workers of the Dorvast railway repair yards, and its nickname, "the Engines", still refers to those origins. The club colours are green and black.

FC Dorvast plays its home matches at Belkarin Arena on the western edge of town. The club has won the national championship three times, in 1977, 1986 and 2008, and the national cup twice. Its youth academy is regarded as one of the best in the country and has produced several international players.

The head coach of FC Dorvast is Ruslan Imangali, a former club captain who returned to the club as coach after managing in the second division. He is known for a high-pressing style and for giving academy players early chances in the first team.
