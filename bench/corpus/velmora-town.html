Velmora is a town in the western part of Darvesh Province, on the left bank of the Isvelka River. The town was founded in 1764 by salt traders who used the river ford as a staging post for caravans carrying rock salt from the Kumdala Hills to the northern markets. The old salt warehouses along Quay Street are still standing and now house shops, a small brewery and the Velmora Salt Museum.

As of the 2019 census the population of Velmora is 18,460. The town is the second largest settlement in the province after the provincial capital, Saryzhal. Most residents live in the old town on the riverbank and in the newer districts of Lindenhof and Upper Ford that grew up around the railway station in the 1960s.

Economy

For most of the twentieth century the economy of Velmora was built on glass. Sand from the Isvelka terraces proved ideal for optical glass, and the optics manufacturer that bears the town's name remains the largest private employer. Food processing, river freight and a growing number of small software firms make up the rest of the local economy.

Government

The town is governed by an elected council of fifteen members and a directly elected mayor. The current mayor, Kirill Ostanek, was first elected on a platform of restoring the riverside promenade and extending the tram line to Upper Ford.

Culture

Every September the town holds the Salt Fair, a weekend market where traders dress in eighteenth-century costume. The Velmora Salt Museum, opened in the former customs house, displays scales, wagons and ledgers from the caravan period.
