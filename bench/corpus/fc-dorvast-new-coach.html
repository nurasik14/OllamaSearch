FC Dorvast name Pieter Vanaukas as new head coach

Sports news - 9 January 2024

FC Dorvast have appointed Pieter Vanaukas as their new head coach. The 46-year-old signed a contract until the end of the 2026 season and took charge of his first training session on Tuesday. He replaces Ruslan Imangali, who left the club by mutual agreement after a run of seven matches without a win.

Vanaukas previously coached Kelstrand Rovers, whom he led to promotion to the Premier Division, and worked for several years as an assistant coach abroad. He is known for a possession-based style and for his work with young players.

"Ruslan Imangali gave this club a great deal, as a player and as a coach, and we thank him," the club's president said. "With Pieter Vanaukas we want to start a new chapter." Vanaukas's first match in charge will be the derby away at Saryzhal United later this month.

The appointment comes at a difficult moment. The club, which challenged for the title last season, has slipped to sixth place in the Premier Division and was knocked out of the national cup in the first round. Supporters had called for change after a home defeat in December, when part of the crowd left before the final whistle.

Vanaukas will bring his own assistant coach and fitness coach with him. The club also confirmed that the academy director will stay in his post, and that no major signings are planned before the transfer window closes at the end of the month.
