Halvorsk Marathon 2021: results

The 2021 Halvorsk Marathon was held on Sunday 10 October in cool, dry weather. A total of 6,912 runners finished the full marathon, which follows the embankments of the Tobyl-Su canal and finishes in Cathedral Square.

The men's race was won by Bekzat Orynbai in 2:11:08, after he broke away from the leading group at the 35-kilometre mark. The women's race was won by Lidia Szaremba in 2:27:44, a new course record.

The course is known as one of the flattest in the region. It starts on the University Bridge, runs twice around the old town along the canal embankments, crosses into the eastern districts and returns along the river park before the final kilometre on the cobbles of Cathedral Square. Spectators lined the route in large numbers, and live bands played at twelve points along the way.

Alongside the marathon, about 9,000 people took part in the half marathon, the ten-kilometre race and the children's run. Runners raised money for the city's children's hospital, which received a record donation.

The organisers thanked more than 800 volunteers and the city of Halvorsk for its support. The 2022 marathon will take place on the second Sunday of October.
