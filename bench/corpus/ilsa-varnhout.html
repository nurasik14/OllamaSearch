Ilsa Varnhout

Ilsa Varnhout is a singer-songwriter from Kelstrand whose music combines folk melodies with electronic production. She began performing in the bars of the old harbour district while studying architecture, and she still writes most of her songs at a piano in her grandmother's flat.

Career

Her debut album, Glass Orchards, was released in 2014 on a small independent label. The album's single "Salt on the Window" became a surprise radio hit, and the record won the regional music prize for best debut. Varnhout's second album, Lowland Choir (2018), featured a choir of thirty amateur singers recorded in a disused church.

Her third album, Weather for Two, appeared in 2021 and was followed by her largest tour to date. She is known for elaborate stage lighting and for inviting local choirs on stage at her concerts.

Style

Varnhout's songs are built around simple piano figures and layered vocals, often recorded in unusual spaces such as stairwells, empty warehouses and churches. Critics have praised her lyrics about coastal towns, family history and the changing seasons. Her live concerts are known for slow, dramatic lighting changes designed together with her former architecture classmates, and she has headlined festivals across the coast and the inland provinces.
